Website Evaluations

The following evaluations compare two websites using CRAP principles of design: Contrast, Repetition, Alignment, and Proximity. Architectural Digest is evaluated as a good example of effective web design, while New Leaf Behavioral Health is evaluated as an example of bad web design.

Architectural Digest

Architectural Digest is a lifestyle magazine website that showcases interior design and architecture.

  • Contrast: AD uses differences in font size, shapes, and imagery to make important headings and certain information stand out. The contrast helps visitors identify the main content quickly.
  • Repetition: The website consistently uses familiar font, background colors, and image styles across many pages. This repetition creates a unified design.
  • Alignment: Headings, product images, descriptions, and buttons are arranged along clear horizontal and vertical lines which make it look organized and professional.
  • Proximity: Images, text, and buttons are generally grouped together. Spacing separates different information into sections, making information easier to understand.

New Leaf Behavioral Health

New Leaf Behavioral Health is an website that provides treatment plans for patients looking for behavioral health services.

  • Contrast: The large number of text elements and visual details makes it difficult to determine which information is most important. The logo and title are slighly blurry which makes the site look less professional. There is also not enough contrast between text and background colors, which can make reading difficult and all elements hard to distinguish.
  • Repetition: While the font is consistent, the alignment and colors are not. The contact information box is a different color than the other boxes which makes it look out of place.
  • Alignment: The crowded arrangement of product images and text weakens the alignment. The input box is overlapping the first information box. A consistent grid with clear columns would make the website easier to look through.
  • Proximity: The elements are not spaces out evenly. There is a lot of empty space on the sides, and the small font size makes the site look even more congested.
